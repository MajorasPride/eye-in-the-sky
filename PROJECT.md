# Eye in the Sky (working title) — handoff

Owner: MajorasPride. Tracker ID: P050.

## What it is
A Left 4 Dead-style zombie RTS where you cannot control the survivors. They decide for
themselves; you are the eye in the sky backing them up: mark spots Safe or Danger, scan for
guns, ammo and meds, snipe single zombies, throw flares. Later levels add the helicopter
strike (hurts survivors too), raising Tower Bridge, and stadium lights that stun zombies.

- Map 1: Sacramento, 4 levels: Highway 50 overpasses, downtown, past the river, stadium.
- Finale: L4D-style holdout at the stadium (short calm to set up, 90 seconds of waves,
  2 Tank zombies, then run for the chopper). Not a single boss fight.
- Survivors: Rosa heals, Duke charges in, Kai runs when scared, Walt only listens once he
  trusts you. Good calls raise trust, bad calls lower it. Dead stays dead for the run.
